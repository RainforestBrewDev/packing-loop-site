Help test Packing Loop (もちものループ), a reusable packing checklist for work, travel, the gym and everyday routines.

Released on 23 October 2026. Test countries: Japan, United Kingdom, United States, Canada and Australia.

Packing Loop Testers and AndroidClosedJP are configured for test access. Version 1.2.0 is now published to the closed test. Join either group, then opt in on Google Play using the same Google account. If the participation page is unavailable, follow the account and country checks below or contact us by email.

Before you join

  • Use an Android phone running Android 8.0 or later, with a Google Play account in one of the five test countries.
  • Use the same Google account for Google Groups, the test participation page and the Play Store.
  • If you already joined the app's internal test, leave that internal test first. Then join the closed test.
  • Be willing to remain opted in for at least 14 consecutive days and actually try the app. No public review, rating or reciprocal-testing commitment is required.

Join in three steps

  1. Join Packing Loop Testers

    This is the app's own international access group. English and Japanese speakers are welcome.

    Join the tester group

    If you already belong to AndroidClosedJP, that existing access route remains enabled. You do not need to leave it. Group membership alone does not enrol you in the Play test.

  2. Opt in to the Google Play closed test

    After joining the group, open the participation page and choose Become a tester.

    Join the Play test

    If the page says the app is unavailable, check your signed-in account, group membership and Play country. New settings can take time to appear.

  3. Install from Google Play

    Packing Loop is a paid, one-time purchase app. To volunteer without paying, request a test code by email first. Codes are limited and are shared privately; a code is not guaranteed. Redeem an issued code with the same Google account, then install through Google Play.

    Request a test code

    Open Packing Loop on Google Play

    The current test-code batch expires on 30 November 2026. If you intend to use a free code, wait for it before completing a paid purchase. Joining the test does not make the app free automatically.

What to try

  • Create a sample list from a template, add an item and edit its name.
  • Long-press the drag handle to reorder checklist items and lists on the My lists screen. Try moving near the top or bottom of a long list to scroll, and try the menu's move-up and move-down options too.
  • Close and reopen the app to check that both list and item order are retained. Export and restore a sample backup, then check the order again. Back up important lists before restoring a backup.
  • Check a few items, show unchecked items, complete the list and reset it for another use.
  • Duplicate a list, export a backup and try sharing a sample checklist as text. Keep a backup of any important lists.
  • Tell us about confusing English or Japanese wording, display problems or unexpected behaviour. Include the Android version and steps to reproduce; avoid personal data.

Send private feedback

The 14-day participation period starts when you opt in on Google Play, not when you join the group or email us. Remain opted in throughout the test. An APK installed outside Google Play does not replace the Play opt-in step. Actual participation and feedback inform the production-access application.

国際版のクローズドテストは日本・英国・米国・カナダ・豪州が対象です。Android 8.0以降の端末で、Googleグループ・参加ページ・Playストアに同じGoogleアカウントを使用してください。

  1. Packing Loop Testersに参加します。既存のAndroidClosedJP経由の参加も維持しています。
  2. Google Playのテスト参加ページで「テスターになる」を選びます。内部テストに参加済みの場合は、先に内部テストから退出してください。

Packing Loop TestersとAndroidClosedJPの両グループをテスト参加対象に設定し、バージョン1.2.0を公開しました。いずれかのグループに参加した後、同じGoogleアカウントでPlayの参加手続きを行ってください。参加ページを開けない場合は、ログイン中のアカウント・グループへの参加状況・Playの国設定をご確認のうえ、解決しなければメールでお知らせください。

グループ参加だけではPlayのテスト参加になりません。Playで参加してから14日間連続で参加状態を維持し、実際に使って気づいた点をお知らせください。公開レビュー・評価・相互テストの約束は不要です。現在のコードの有効期限は2026年11月30日です。

通勤や旅行のサンプルリストを作成し、項目の編集、チェック、未チェック表示、リセット、複製、バックアップ書き出し、テキスト共有をお試しください。

  • 持ち物と「マイリスト」の並べ替えハンドルを長押しして、ドラッグで順序を変更してください。長い一覧の上下端での自動スクロールと、メニューからの上下移動もお試しください。
  • アプリを閉じて開き直した後も、持ち物・マイリストの順序が保持されることをご確認ください。サンプルのバックアップを書き出して読み込み、順序が保持されることもお試しください。読み込む前に、大切なリストのバックアップを保存してください。

不具合や分かりにくい表現は rainforestbrewdev@gmail.com へお送りください。

Privacy and support

The app stores checklists on your device and has no Internet permission, ads, sign-in or analytics. You choose where exported files or shared text go. The tester group is only for test access and coordination; it does not upload your checklists.

Google processes your group membership, profile and Play participation under its own policies. The member list is restricted to the group's managers and owner; group conversations are visible to members. Send test-code requests and personal feedback privately by email. Do not post passwords, verification codes, payment details, promotional codes or other personal information to the group. Emails are used to coordinate access and respond to feedback, and are not published.
